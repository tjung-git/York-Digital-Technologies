Seoul

Location

Seoul is located on the banks of the Han River in the northwestern part of South Korea.

About

History

Founded over 2,000 years ago, Seoul has been the political and cultural heart of Korea.

Culture

Seoul blends ancient traditions with pop culture, from historic palaces to the global phenomenon of Kpop and Korean TV dramas.

Landmarks

Iconic sites include Historic Gyeongbokgung Palace, the modern Dongdaemun Design Plaza, and the panoramic views from the Nam San Tower

Things To Do

  • Explore the royal grounds of Gyeongbokgung Palace.
  • Shop and street-food/bar hop in Myeongdong.
  • Hike the scenic trails of various national parks.
  • Wander through the Bukchon Hanok Village.
